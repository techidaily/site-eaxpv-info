### Key Takeaways

* You can use the new AI tool on iPhone by switching to the "Gemini" tab in the Google app.
* Users can easily hide Gemini from the Google app by toggling off "Show Gemini" in settings.

 Gemini is Google's latest and greatest AI tool. A combination of Google Bard and Duet AI, it's set to rival ChatGPT and DALL-E 3\. You don't need an Android phone or a desktop browser to try it out. We'll show you how to use Gemini AI on your iPhone or iPad.

 As of the time of writing, there is not a dedicated Gemini app for iPhone and iPad. Instead, it's available in the official Google app. [Gemini is free to use](https://location-social.techidaily.com/does-find-my-friends-work-on-vivo-x90s-drfone-by-drfone-virtual-android/), but you can upgrade to [Gemini Advanced](https://gemini.google.com/advanced?utm%5Fsource=gemini&utm%5Fmedium=web&utm%5Fcampaign=gemini%5Fadvanced%5Fannounce%5Fsh) for $20 per month for more, well, _advanced_ features.

 First, open the Google app and look for "Gemini" in the top tabs. Select it to switch to Gemini.

##  How to Remove Gemini From the Google App on iPhone

 If you don't like Gemini showing up in the [Google app](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/google/id284815942), you can choose to hide it. First, open the app and tap your profile icon in the top corner. Select "Settings" from the menu.

![Google app menu on iPhone.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/img_0399-1.PNG) 

 Next, go to "Voice."

![Google app settings on iPhone.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/img_0400.PNG) 

 Toggle off "Show Gemini" and tap "Done."

![Gemini option in the Google app settings on iPhone.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/img_0401.PNG)
