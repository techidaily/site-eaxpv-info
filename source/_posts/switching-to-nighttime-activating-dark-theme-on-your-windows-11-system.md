##  Enable Dark Mode on Windows 11

 In Windows 11, you can activate dark mode by toggling on the option in the Settings app.

 To do that, first, open the [Settings app](https://easy-unlock-android.techidaily.com/in-2024-how-to-show-wi-fi-password-on-oneplus-nord-n30-se-by-drfone-android/) on your PC. Do this by pressing the Windows+i keys at the same time.

 On the Settings screen, from the sidebar to the left, select "Personalization."

![Select "Personalization" in Settings on Windows 11.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/1-settings-personalization.png)

 On the "Personalization" screen, from the options on the right pane, choose "Colors."

![Select "Colors" in Windows 11's Settings app.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/2-color-settings.png) 

 The Colors screen will open. Here, click the "Choose Your Mode" drop-down menu and select "Dark."

![Select "Dark" in Settings on Windows 11.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/3-dark-mode-windows-11.png)

 And instantly, Windows 11 will enable dark mode on your entire PC. The Settings page you're on will also turn darkish.

 To get an even better dark mode experience, change your PC's theme to a darker one. You can do this by going into the "[Personalization](https://buynow-help.techidaily.com/expert-picks-best-portable-wireless-routers-of-2024/)" menu on the Settings screen.

![Click "Personalization" in Settings on Windows 11.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/4-personalization-settings.png) 

 At the top of the Personalization screen, from under the "Select a Theme to Apply" section, choose the "Windows (Dark)" theme.

![Select the "Windows (dark)" theme in Settings on Windows 11.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/5-dark-theme-windows-11.png) 

 And Windows 11 will apply the selected dark theme, turning pretty much everything on the PC darker!

##  Disable Dark Mode on Windows 11

 To disable dark mode and go back to light mode, head into Settings > Personalization > Colors. Then, click the "Choose Your Mode" drop-down menu and select "Light."

![Select "Light" in Settings on Windows 11.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/7-light-mode-windows-11.png) 

 Click "Personalization" in the left sidebar, and then choose the "Windows (Light)" theme from the top.

![Select the "Windows (light)" theme in Settings on Windows 11.](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/8-light-theme-windows-11.png) 

 And your PC is back to the original Windows 11 light mode!
